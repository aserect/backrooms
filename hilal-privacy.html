🌙 Hilal. - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Hilal. is a prayer-streak app made to help Muslims pray their five daily salahs. Your privacy matters to us, and the app is built to keep your data on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything personal: user names, streaks, prayer check-ins, profile photos, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded anywhere. We have no servers, no accounts, and no access to any of it.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location for two things only: calculating accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction. To get prayer times, your approximate coordinates are sent to the free AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) - a prayer-times service. No name or identifier is sent with them, and we do not store them. You can deny location access and the app still works for tracking prayers manually.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, prayer-time reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing about them leaves your phone.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising
  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • No selling or sharing of data - we never see your data at all

Children

The app supports kid profiles. All of a child's data stays on the family's own device, like everyone else's. We collect nothing from anyone, children included.
