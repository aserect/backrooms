🌙 Hilal. - Support

Hilal. helps you pray all five daily salahs: build streaks, get prayer-time reminders for your city, find the Qibla with a live compass, and learn how to pray step by step with audio recitation.

Common questions

Prayer times look wrong?

Make sure Location is allowed (Settings > Hilal. > Location) and open the app once while online - times refresh each time you open it.

No notifications?

Allow notifications in Settings > Hilal. > Notifications, and open the app at least once a day so the next days' reminders get scheduled.

The compass needle is jumpy?

Hold the phone flat and wave it in a figure-8 to calibrate.
